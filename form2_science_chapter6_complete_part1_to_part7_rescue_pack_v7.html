Properties of Acids and Alkalis

English is the main examination language. Chinese explanations are included only to help weaker students understand the scientific meaning.

1. What Is an Acid?

1Acid

An acid is a substance that shows acidic properties when dissolved in water.

酸是溶解在水中后会表现酸性特征的物质。

Examples: Hydrochloric acid, sulphuric acid, nitric acid, ethanoic acid.

2. Properties of Acids

PropertyExam Statement华语辅助
TasteAcids taste sour.酸有酸味。
Litmus paperAcids turn blue litmus paper red.酸使蓝色石蕊试纸变红。
CorrosivenessStrong acids may be corrosive.强酸可能具有腐蚀性。
Reaction with metalsAcids react with certain metals to produce hydrogen gas.酸与某些金属反应会产生氢气。
Reaction with carbonatesAcids react with carbonates to produce carbon dioxide gas.酸与碳酸盐反应会产生二氧化碳。
Acid: Sour • Blue litmus turns Red • Can react with metals

3. What Is an Alkali?

2Alkali

An alkali is a base that dissolves in water.

碱是能够溶解在水中的碱性物质。

Examples: Sodium hydroxide solution, potassium hydroxide solution, ammonia solution.

4. Properties of Alkalis

PropertyExam Statement华语辅助
TasteAlkalis taste bitter.碱有苦味。
TouchAlkalis feel soapy or slippery.碱摸起来像肥皂般滑。
Litmus paperAlkalis turn red litmus paper blue.碱使红色石蕊试纸变蓝。
CorrosivenessStrong alkalis may be corrosive.强碱可能具有腐蚀性。
Alkali: Bitter • Slippery • Red litmus turns Blue

5. Acid and Alkali Comparison

FeatureAcidAlkali

Memory Trick:
Acid attacks BLUE and makes it RED.

华语口诀：酸：蓝变红；碱：红变蓝。

6. The Role of Water

Acids and alkalis show their characteristic properties only when dissolved in water.

酸和碱只有溶解在水中后，才会表现各自的特征。

Exam idea: Dry hydrogen chloride gas does not change dry blue litmus paper because water is absent.
No water → No acidic or alkaline properties shown clearly

7. Reaction of Acid with Metal

Acid + Metal → Salt + Hydrogen

酸 + 金属 → 盐 + 氢气

ObservationInference
Bubbles are produced.A gas is produced.
The gas produces a “pop” sound with a lighted splint.The gas is hydrogen.
The metal becomes smaller.The metal reacts with the acid.
Acid + Metal = Hydrogen gives a “pop” sound

8. Safety Rules

Do not taste laboratory chemicals.
Do not touch acids or alkalis directly.
Wear safety goggles and gloves.
Wash spills with plenty of water and inform the teacher.
Use only small quantities during experiments.
Never smell chemicals directly.

9. Common Examination Traps

Wrong: Acid turns red litmus blue.
Correct: Acid turns blue litmus red.

Wrong: Alkali turns blue litmus red.
Correct: Alkali turns red litmus blue.

Wrong: All acids are safe because some occur in food.
Correct: Strong acids can be corrosive and dangerous.

Wrong: Acids show their properties without water.
Correct: Water is needed for acids and alkalis to show their characteristic properties.

An indicator is a substance that changes colour in acidic or alkaline conditions.

指示剂是在酸性或碱性环境中会改变颜色的物质。

Part 3: Neutralisation

English is the main examination language. Chinese explanations are included only to support understanding.

1Definition

Neutralisation is a reaction between an acid and an alkali to produce salt and water.

中和反应是酸与碱反应，产生盐和水的过程。

Acid + Alkali → Salt + Water

2. Example of Neutralisation

Hydrochloric acid + Sodium hydroxide → Sodium chloride + Water

盐酸 + 氢氧化钠 → 氯化钠 + 水

SubstanceRole华语辅助
Hydrochloric acidAcid酸
Sodium hydroxideAlkali碱
Sodium chlorideSalt盐
WaterProduct产物

3. Changes During Neutralisation

Acidity decreases.
Alkalinity decreases.
The pH moves towards 7.
Salt is formed.
Water is formed.
Heat is usually released.
Memory Trick: Acid meets alkali → Both lose strength → Salt + Water

4. Temperature Change

Neutralisation is usually an exothermic reaction.

Observation: The temperature of the mixture increases.

Inference: Heat energy is released.

中和反应通常是放热反应。观察：混合物温度上升。推断：热能被释放。

Temperature increases → Heat released → Exothermic reaction

5. Indicators and End Point

An indicator is used to show when neutralisation is complete.

指示剂用来显示中和反应何时完成。

IndicatorUseful Colour Information
PhenolphthaleinPink in alkali; colourless in acid or neutral solution.
Methyl orangeRed in acid; yellow in alkali.
Universal indicatorGreen near pH 7.
End point: The point at which the indicator changes colour permanently.
华语：终点是指示剂出现持久颜色变化的时刻。

6. Titration Apparatus

ApparatusFunction华语辅助
BuretteAdds one solution slowly and accurately.缓慢且准确地加入溶液。
PipetteMeasures a fixed volume accurately.准确量取固定体积。
Conical flaskContains the reacting solution and can be swirled safely.装反应溶液，并方便摇匀。
IndicatorShows the end point by a colour change.通过颜色变化显示终点。

7. Basic Titration Procedure

  1. Measure the alkali using a pipette.
  2. Transfer it into a conical flask.
  3. Add a few drops of indicator.
  4. Add acid slowly from the burette.
  5. Swirl the flask continuously.
  6. Stop when the indicator changes colour permanently.

用移液管量取碱液，放入锥形瓶，加入指示剂，再从滴定管缓慢加入酸，同时摇匀；当颜色永久改变时停止。

Pipette → Flask → Indicator → Burette → Swirl → End point

8. Observation, Inference and Conclusion

Science SkillCorrect Answer
ObservationThe indicator changes colour permanently.
ObservationThe temperature increases.
InferenceNeutralisation has reached the end point.
InferenceHeat energy is released.
ConclusionAn acid reacts with an alkali to form salt and water.

9. Common Examination Traps

Wrong: Acid + Alkali → Hydrogen + Water
Correct: Acid + Alkali → Salt + Water

Wrong: A temperature increase means heat is absorbed.
Correct: A temperature increase means heat is released.

Wrong: The burette measures a fixed volume for transfer.
Correct: The pipette measures a fixed volume; the burette adds solution gradually.

Wrong: Any temporary colour change is the end point.
Correct: The colour change must remain permanent.

Part 4: Applications of Neutralisation

English is the main examination language. Chinese explanations are included only to support understanding.

1Core Exam Idea

Neutralisation is used to reduce excessive acidity or alkalinity in daily life.

中和反应用来降低日常生活中过强的酸性或碱性。

Identify the problem → Choose the opposite substance → Neutralise

2. Toothpaste and Tooth Decay

Bacteria in the mouth produce acids that may damage tooth enamel.

Alkaline toothpaste neutralises the acids produced by bacteria.

口腔细菌会产生酸，酸可能侵蚀牙釉质。碱性牙膏可以中和这些酸。

Mouth acid + Alkaline toothpaste → Less tooth decay

3. Antacid and Excess Stomach Acid

The stomach contains hydrochloric acid for digestion.

Antacid contains a mild alkali that neutralises excess stomach acid.

胃中含有帮助消化的盐酸。抗酸剂含有温和的碱，可中和过多的胃酸。

Exam sentence: Antacid neutralises excess hydrochloric acid in the stomach.

4. Acidic Soil

Soil that is too acidic may reduce healthy plant growth.

Slaked lime is added to acidic soil to reduce its acidity.

酸性过强的土壤可能不利于植物健康生长。加入熟石灰可以降低土壤酸性。

Acidic soil → Add slaked lime

5. Acidic Industrial Waste

Some factories produce acidic wastewater.

An alkali is added before discharge to neutralise the acid and reduce environmental harm.

部分工厂会产生酸性废水。排放前加入碱来中和酸性，以减少环境危害。

Strong answer: An alkali is added to acidic industrial wastewater before discharge to reduce its acidity.

6. Bee Sting and Wasp Sting

Sting Nature of Venom Substance Used 华语辅助
Bee sting Acidic A mild alkali 蜜蜂毒液呈酸性，可用温和的碱中和。
Wasp sting Alkaline A weak acid 黄蜂毒液呈碱性，可用弱酸中和。
Memory Trick:
Bee = Acid → Use alkali
Wasp = Alkali → Use acid

7. Hair Conditioner, Facial Toner and Fabric Softener

Product Application 华语辅助
Hair conditioner Mildly acidic conditioner neutralises alkaline residue left by shampoo. 弱酸性护发素中和洗发水留下的碱性残留。
Facial toner May help neutralise alkaline facial cleanser residue and restore skin pH. 可帮助中和碱性洁面产品残留，并恢复皮肤 pH。
Fabric softener Helps neutralise alkaline detergent residue on clothes. 帮助中和衣物上的碱性清洁剂残留。

Part 5: Experiments and Science Process Skills

This section focuses on experiment questions, variables, observations, inferences and conclusions.

1SPS Core Formula

Manipulated Variable: The factor that is changed.

Responding Variable: The factor that is measured or observed.

Controlled Variable: The factor that is kept constant.

操纵变量：主动改变的因素。反应变量：被测量或观察的结果。控制变量：保持不变的因素。

MV = Change • RV = Measure • CV = Keep the same
