1. Three States of Water

Students must identify each state correctly in English.

1Solid

Ice is water in the solid state.

冰是水的固体状态。

2Liquid

3Gas

Water vapour is water in the gaseous state.

水蒸气是水的气体状态。

Part 2: Solution

English is the main examination language. Chinese explanations are included only to support understanding.

1Solute

A solute is a substance that dissolves in a solvent.

溶质是溶解在溶剂中的物质。

Example: Sugar in sugar solution.

2Solvent

A solvent is a liquid that dissolves a solute.

溶剂是用来溶解溶质的液体。

Example: Water in sugar solution.

3Solution

A solution is a uniform mixture formed when a solute dissolves in a solvent.

溶液是溶质溶解在溶剂中后形成的均匀混合物。

Solute + Solvent = Solution

2. Soluble and Insoluble Substances

Term Meaning 华语辅助
Soluble A substance that can dissolve in a solvent. 可以溶解在溶剂中的物质。
Insoluble A substance that cannot dissolve in a solvent. 不能溶解在溶剂中的物质。
Memory Trick: Soluble = can dissolve. Insoluble = cannot dissolve.

3. Dilute, Concentrated and Saturated Solutions

Dilute Solution

Contains a small amount of solute compared to the amount of solvent.

相对于溶剂，含有较少量的溶质。

Concentrated Solution

Contains a large amount of solute compared to the amount of solvent.

相对于溶剂，含有较大量的溶质。

Saturated Solution

A solution that cannot dissolve any more solute at a particular temperature.

在特定温度下，已经不能再溶解更多溶质的溶液。

Exam clue: Extra solute remains undissolved at the bottom.
Dilute Concentrated Saturated
Small amount of solute Large amount of solute Cannot dissolve more solute
较少溶质 较多溶质 不能再溶解更多溶质

4. Factors Affecting the Rate of Dissolving

These factors affect how fast a solute dissolves, not how much can dissolve.

Higher Temperature

Solute particles and solvent particles move faster and collide more frequently.

溶质与溶剂粒子移动得更快，碰撞更频繁，因此溶解速度更快。

Hotter → Faster particles → Faster dissolving

Stirring

Stirring brings fresh solvent into contact with the solute more quickly.

搅拌让新的溶剂更快接触溶质，因此溶解更快。

Stir → More contact → Faster dissolving

Smaller Size of Solute

Smaller particles have a larger total surface area exposed to the solvent.

较小的颗粒拥有更大的总表面积与溶剂接触，因此溶解更快。

Smaller size → Larger surface area → Faster dissolving
Do not confuse: Rate of dissolving means how fast. Solubility means how much can dissolve.

5. Solution, Suspension and Colloid

Feature Solution Suspension Colloid
Appearance Uniform and transparent Cloudy Cloudy but stable
Particles settle? No Yes No
Can be separated by filtration? No Yes No
Example Salt water Muddy water Milk
华语 溶液 悬浮液 胶体
Memory Trick:
Solution = clear and does not settle.
Suspension = settles and can be filtered.
Colloid = cloudy but does not settle.

6. Water as a Universal Solvent

Water is called a universal solvent because it can dissolve many substances.

水被称为通用溶剂，因为它能够溶解许多物质。

Important: Universal does not mean water can dissolve everything.

Some substances such as oil do not dissolve in water.

有些物质，例如油，不能溶解在水中。

Part 3: Electrolysis of Water

English is the main examination language. Chinese explanations are included only to support understanding.

1Definition of Electrolysis

Electrolysis is the process of decomposing a compound using electricity.

电解是利用电流把化合物分解的过程。

Electricity breaks a compound apart = Electrolysis

2Products Formed

Water is decomposed into hydrogen gas and oxygen gas.

水被分解成氢气和氧气。

Word equation:
Water → Hydrogen + Oxygen

3Volume Ratio

The volume ratio of hydrogen to oxygen is 2 : 1.

氢气与氧气的体积比例是 2 : 1。

Hydrogen : Oxygen = 2 : 1

2. Electrodes and Gases Produced

Electrode Other Name Gas Produced 华语辅助
Negative electrode Cathode Hydrogen 负极产生氢气
Positive electrode Anode Oxygen 正极产生氧气
Memory Trick:
Negative → Hydrogen
Positive → Oxygen

3. Gas Tests

Hydrogen Test

Method: Place a lighted wooden splint near the gas.

Observation: A “pop” sound is produced.

Inference: Hydrogen gas is present.

方法：把燃烧的木条靠近气体。
观察：发出“啪”声。
推断：存在氢气。

Hydrogen = Lighted splint + “Pop” sound

Oxygen Test

Method: Insert a glowing wooden splint into the gas.

Observation: The glowing wooden splint relights.

Inference: Oxygen gas is present.

方法：把带火星的木条放入气体中。
观察：木条重新燃烧。
推断：存在氧气。

Oxygen = Glowing splint relights
Do not mix them up:
Hydrogen gives a “pop” sound.
Oxygen relights a glowing wooden splint.

4. Why Dilute Acid Is Added

Pure water is a poor conductor of electricity.

A few drops of dilute acid are added to increase the electrical conductivity of water.

纯水导电能力弱。加入少量稀酸是为了提高水的导电性。

Dilute acid → Better conductivity → Electrolysis can occur more effectively

5. Observation and Inference

Observation Inference
More gas is collected at the negative electrode. Hydrogen is produced in a larger volume.
The gas at the negative electrode produces a “pop” sound. The gas is hydrogen.
The gas at the positive electrode relights a glowing splint. The gas is oxygen.
The volume of hydrogen is twice the volume of oxygen. Hydrogen and oxygen are produced in a 2 : 1 volume ratio.
Exam Rule: Observation = what you see, hear or measure. Inference = the scientific explanation.

6. Common Examination Traps

Do not write oxygen at the negative electrode.
Do not write the ratio as 1 : 2.
Do not use a glowing splint for hydrogen.
Do not write “pop” sound for oxygen.
Remember that pure water conducts electricity poorly.
Write observation and inference separately.

Part 4: Water Purification and Water Treatment

English is the main examination language. Chinese explanations are included only to support understanding.

1Why Water Must Be Treated

Raw water may contain suspended particles, microorganisms, dissolved substances and unpleasant odours.

未经处理的水可能含有悬浮颗粒、微生物、溶解物质和异味。

Water treatment makes water clean and safer for use.

2. Main Water Treatment Sequence

The correct sequence must be memorised.

Screening筛除
→
Coagulation混凝
→
Sedimentation沉淀
Sedimentation沉淀
→
Filtration过滤
→
Chlorination加氯消毒
Memory Sequence:
Screening → Coagulation → Sedimentation → Filtration → Chlorination

3. Function of Each Treatment Step

Step Main Function 华语辅助
Screening Removes large objects such as leaves, branches and rubbish. 去除树叶、树枝和垃圾等大型杂物。
Coagulation Alum is added to make small suspended particles clump together. 加入明矾，让细小悬浮颗粒凝聚成较大的颗粒。
Sedimentation Heavier clumps settle at the bottom of the tank. 较重的颗粒沉到水槽底部。
Filtration Removes remaining suspended particles using layers such as sand and gravel. 利用沙和碎石等过滤层去除剩余悬浮颗粒。
Chlorination Chlorine is added to kill harmful microorganisms. 加入氯来杀死有害微生物。

4. Important Materials

Alum

Alum causes small suspended particles to clump together into larger particles.

明矾使细小悬浮颗粒凝聚成较大的颗粒。

Alum gathers particles.

Chlorine

Chlorine kills harmful microorganisms in water.

氯杀死水中的有害微生物。

Chlorine kills microorganisms.

Sand

Sand traps smaller suspended particles during filtration.

沙在过滤过程中拦截较小的悬浮颗粒。

Gravel

Gravel supports the filter layers and traps larger particles.

碎石支撑过滤层，并拦截较大的颗粒。

5. Alum and Chlorine Comparison

Substance Function Common Wrong Idea
Alum Causes small particles to clump together. Alum does not mainly kill microorganisms.
Chlorine Kills harmful microorganisms. Chlorine does not remove large rubbish.
Memory Trick: Alum gathers, chlorine kills.
华语口诀：明矾聚颗粒，氯杀微生物。

6. Examination Keywords

Small particles clump → Coagulation

Sand and gravel → Filtration
Kill microorganisms → Chlorination
Alum → Coagulation

7. Common Examination Traps

Do not write:
Alum kills bacteria.

Filtration kills all microorganisms.

Correct ideas:
Alum gathers particles.
Chlorine kills microorganisms.

3. Function of Each Part

Part Main Function 华语辅助
Reservoir Stores raw water before treatment. 储存处理前的原水。
Water Treatment Plant Treats raw water to make it cleaner and safer for use. 处理原水，使水更清洁、更安全。

Raw water is untreated water collected from natural sources.

Treated Water

Treated water has passed through the water treatment process.

处理水是已经经过净水程序的水。

Stored in: Storage tank

5. Why a Storage Tank Is Important

Stores treated water before distribution.
Helps maintain a continuous water supply.

Helps distribute water to different areas.

储水槽储存处理后的水，帮助维持持续供水，并在短暂中断时提供备用水。

Part 6: Water Pollution and Water Sustainability

English is the main examination language. Chinese explanations are included only to support understanding.

1Definition of Water Pollution

Water pollution is the contamination of water by harmful substances.

水污染是水被有害物质污染。

Harmful substances enter water → Water quality decreases

2. Main Causes of Water Pollution

Domestic Waste

Examples include rubbish, detergent and sewage.

例子包括垃圾、清洁剂和污水。

Industrial Waste

Examples include chemicals, toxic waste and heavy metals.

例子包括化学物质、有毒废料和重金属。

Agricultural Activities

Fertilisers, pesticides and animal waste may enter rivers.

肥料、农药和动物粪便可能流入河流。

Oil Spills

Oil forms a layer on the water surface and reduces oxygen exchange.

油会在水面形成油膜，并减少氧气交换。

3. Effects of Water Pollution

Effect Scientific Explanation 华语辅助
Death of aquatic organisms Pollutants reduce water quality and may poison aquatic organisms. 污染物降低水质，并可能毒害水生生物。
Reduced dissolved oxygen Oil films and excessive algae may reduce the oxygen available in water. 油膜和过量藻类可能减少水中的溶解氧。
Spread of disease Contaminated water may contain harmful microorganisms. 受污染的水可能含有有害微生物并传播疾病。
Unpleasant smell and appearance Polluted water may become dirty, smelly and unsuitable for use. 受污染的水可能肮脏、有异味，并不适合使用。

4. Eutrophication

Memory Trick:

华语口诀：肥料多 → 藻类多 → 氧气少 → 鱼死亡

5. Ways to Reduce Water Pollution

Dispose of rubbish and chemicals properly.
Treat sewage before discharge.
Treat industrial wastewater before discharge.
Reduce excessive use of fertilisers and pesticides.
Enforce environmental laws.
Educate the public about protecting water sources.

应妥善处理垃圾和化学物质，排放前处理污水，减少过量使用肥料和农药，并加强执法与公众教育。

6. Water Sustainability

Water sustainability means using and managing water wisely so that enough clean water remains for future generations.

水资源永续是明智地使用和管理水资源，确保未来世代仍有足够的清洁水。

Use water wisely today → Enough clean water tomorrow

7. Ways to Conserve Water

Action 华语辅助
Turn off the tap when not in use.不用水时关掉水龙头。
Repair leaking pipes and taps.修理漏水的水管和水龙头。
Reuse water when suitable.在适当情况下重复使用水。
Collect rainwater.收集雨水。
Use water-efficient appliances.使用节水设备。
Avoid unnecessary water wastage.避免不必要的水资源浪费。

8. Cause, Effect and Solution

Cause Effect Solution
Untreated sewage enters a river. Water may contain harmful microorganisms and spread disease. Treat sewage before discharge.
Excess fertiliser enters a lake. Algal growth increases and dissolved oxygen decreases. Reduce excessive fertiliser use.
Oil spills on the water surface. Oxygen exchange decreases and aquatic organisms are affected. Prevent spills and clean them quickly.
Factories discharge toxic chemicals. Water quality decreases and organisms may be poisoned. Treat industrial wastewater and enforce laws.

9. Common Examination Traps

Weak answer: Water becomes dirty.
Better answer: Pollutants reduce water quality and may kill aquatic organisms.

Weak answer: Fertiliser kills fish.
Better answer: Excess fertiliser causes rapid algal growth. Decomposition reduces dissolved oxygen, causing fish to die.

Weak answer: Save water.
Better answer: Repair leaking pipes, turn off unused taps and reuse water when suitable.

Experiment 1: Rate of Dissolving

Item Answer 华语辅助
Aim To investigate the effect of temperature on the rate of dissolving. 研究温度对溶解速度的影响。
Manipulated Variable Temperature of water 水的温度
Responding Variable Time taken for the solute to dissolve 溶质完全溶解所需时间
Controlled Variables Amount of water, amount of solute, type of solute 水量、溶质量、溶质种类
Hypothesis The higher the temperature of water, the shorter the time taken for the solute to dissolve. 水温越高，溶质完全溶解所需时间越短。
Conclusion A higher water temperature increases the rate of dissolving. 较高的水温会提高溶解速度。
Higher temperature → Shorter time → Faster dissolving

Experiment 2: Electrolysis of Water

Item Answer 华语辅助
Aim To identify the gases produced during the electrolysis of water. 鉴定水电解时产生的气体。
Observation at Negative Electrode More gas is collected and it produces a “pop” sound. 收集到较多气体，并发出“啪”声。
Inference The gas is hydrogen. 该气体是氢气。
Observation at Positive Electrode The gas relights a glowing wooden splint. 气体使带火星的木条重新燃烧。
Inference The gas is oxygen. 该气体是氧气。
Conclusion Water is decomposed into hydrogen and oxygen in a 2 : 1 volume ratio. 水被分解成氢气和氧气，体积比例为 2 : 1。
Negative = Hydrogen = Pop • Positive = Oxygen = Relight

Experiment 3: Filtration

Item Answer 华语辅助
Aim To remove suspended particles from muddy water by filtration. 利用过滤去除泥水中的悬浮颗粒。
Observation The filtrate is clearer than the original muddy water. 滤液比原本的泥水更清澈。
Inference Suspended particles are trapped by the filter material. 悬浮颗粒被过滤材料拦截。
Limitation Filtration does not remove all dissolved substances or kill all microorganisms. 过滤不能去除所有溶解物质，也不能杀死所有微生物。
Conclusion Filtration removes suspended particles from water. 过滤能去除水中的悬浮颗粒。
Clearer water ≠ Completely safe water
