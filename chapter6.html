Part 1: Properties of Acids and Alkalis

AcidAlkali
SourBitter and slippery

酸：蓝变红；碱：红变蓝。强酸和强碱都可能具有腐蚀性。

Acid + Metal → Salt + Hydrogen

Part 2: Indicators and pH

pHConditionUniversal Indicator
0–6AcidicRed / Orange / Yellow
7NeutralGreen
8–14AlkalineBlue / Purple

Lower pH = stronger acid. Higher pH = stronger alkali.

Phenolphthalein: colourless in acid, pink in alkali.

pH 越低酸性越强；pH 越高碱性越强。

Part 3: Neutralisation

Acid + Alkali → Salt + Water

Neutralisation usually releases heat, so temperature increases.

End point: Permanent indicator colour change.

中和反应产生盐和水，通常是放热反应。指示剂出现持久颜色变化表示达到终点。

Part 5: Experiments and SPS

MV: factor changed. RV: factor measured. CV: factor kept constant.

Observation: what is seen or measured. Inference: scientific explanation.

pH paper gives an approximate value; pH meter gives a more accurate value.

操纵变量＝主动改变；反应变量＝测量结果；控制变量＝保持不变。
