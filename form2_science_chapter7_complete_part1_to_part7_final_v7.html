1. Electricity

Electricity is a form of energy. It can produce light, heat, sound and movement.

电是一种能量，可以产生光、热、声音和运动。

Electricity = Energy

2. Electrical Energy Conversion

3. Electric Charge

There are positive and negative charges. Electrons carry negative charge.

电荷分为正电荷和负电荷。电子带负电。

Electron = Negative charge

4. Electric Current

Electric current is the flow of electric charge.

Unit: Ampere (A)　Instrument: Ammeter

电流是电荷的流动。单位是安培，用电流表测量。

Current = Flow • A • Ammeter

5. Voltage

Voltage is the push that causes electric current to flow.

Unit: Volt (V)　Instrument: Voltmeter

电压是推动电流流动的力量。单位是伏特，用电压表测量。

Voltage = Push • V • Voltmeter

6. Resistance

Resistance opposes the flow of electric current.

Unit: Ohm (Ω)

电阻会阻碍电流流动，单位是欧姆。

Resistance = Oppose • Ω

8. Conductors and Insulators

Conductors

Allow current to flow easily.

Examples: copper, aluminium, iron.

导体容易让电流通过。

Copper → Conduct

Insulators

Do not allow current to flow easily.

Examples: plastic, rubber, glass, dry wood.

绝缘体不容易让电流通过。

Plastic → Protect

Part 2: Electric Circuits

English is the main examination language. Chinese explanations are included only to support understanding.

Open Circuit

An open circuit is incomplete, so electric current cannot flow.

断路是不完整的电路，因此电流不能流动。

Open circuit = No current

Closed Circuit

A closed circuit is complete, so electric current can flow.

闭合电路是完整的电路，因此电流可以流动。

Closed circuit = Current flows

1. Series Circuit

A series circuit has only one path for electric current.

串联电路只有一条电流路径。

Current is the same at every point.
If one bulb breaks, all bulbs go out.
Adding more bulbs usually makes them dimmer.
All components are connected along one path.
Series = Single path

2. Parallel Circuit

A parallel circuit has more than one path for electric current.

并联电路有多条电流路径。

Current splits between branches.
If one bulb breaks, the others can still light.
Bulbs are usually brighter than in a series circuit.
Each branch provides a separate path.
Parallel = Plenty of paths

3. Series and Parallel Comparison

FeatureSeries CircuitParallel Circuit
Number of pathsOneMore than one
CurrentSame throughoutSplits between branches
One bulb breaksAll bulbs go outOther bulbs still work
BrightnessUsually dimmerUsually brighter

4. Conventional Current and Electron Flow

Conventional current: Positive terminal → Negative terminal

Electron flow: Negative terminal → Positive terminal

传统电流方向：正极到负极。电子流方向：负极到正极。

Memory Trick:
Current: + to −
Electron: − to +

6. Why Bulbs Become Dimmer in Series

When more bulbs are added in series, the total resistance increases.

A higher total resistance reduces the current, so the bulbs become dimmer.

串联电路中增加灯泡会提高总电阻，电流减小，因此灯泡变暗。

More bulbs in series → More resistance → Less current → Dimmer bulbs

7. Common Examination Traps

Wrong: Open circuit has current.
Correct: Open circuit is incomplete, so current does not flow.

Wrong: Series circuit has many paths.
Correct: Series circuit has one path.

Wrong: If one parallel bulb breaks, all bulbs go out.
Correct: Other branches can still work.

Wrong: Electron flow and conventional current move in the same direction.
Correct: They move in opposite directions.

Part 3: Current, Voltage and Resistance

English is the main examination language. Chinese explanations are included only to support understanding.

Ammeter

An ammeter measures electric current.

Connection: In series

电流表测量电流，必须串联在电路中。

Ammeter = Ampere = Series

Voltmeter

A voltmeter measures voltage across a component.

Connection: In parallel

电压表测量元件两端的电压，必须并联。

Voltmeter = Voltage = Parallel

1. Current in Series and Parallel Circuits

CircuitRuleFormula华语辅助
Series Current is the same at every point. I₁ = I₂ = I₃ 串联电路中每一点的电流相同。
Parallel Total current is divided among branches. I total = I₁ + I₂ + I₃ 并联电路中，总电流等于各支路电流之和。
Series current stays the same • Parallel current splits

2. Voltage in Series and Parallel Circuits

CircuitRuleFormula华语辅助
Series Total voltage is shared among components. V total = V₁ + V₂ + V₃ 串联电路中，总电压由各元件分担。
Parallel Voltage across each branch is the same. V total = V₁ = V₂ = V₃ 并联电路中，每条支路两端电压相同。
Series voltage shares • Parallel voltage stays the same

3. Resistance and Current

Resistance opposes the flow of electric current.

At the same voltage:

Higher resistance → Lower current
Lower resistance → Higher current

在电压相同的情况下，电阻越大，电流越小；电阻越小，电流越大。

Resistance up → Current down

4. Bulb Brightness

Larger current → Brighter bulb
Smaller current → Dimmer bulb
More bulbs in series → Greater total resistance
Greater total resistance → Smaller current

灯泡亮度通常与电流大小有关。电流较大，灯泡较亮；电流较小，灯泡较暗。

More series bulbs → More resistance → Less current → Dimmer

5. Quantity, Unit and Instrument

QuantityUnitSymbolInstrument
CurrentAmpereAAmmeter
VoltageVoltVVoltmeter
ResistanceOhmΩ—

6. Common Examination Traps

Wrong: Ammeter is connected in parallel.
Correct: Ammeter is connected in series.

Wrong: Voltmeter is connected in series.
Correct: Voltmeter is connected in parallel.

Wrong: Current splits in a series circuit.
Correct: Current is the same throughout a series circuit.

Wrong: Voltage is shared in a parallel circuit.
Correct: Voltage is the same across each parallel branch.

Wrong: Higher resistance gives higher current.
Correct: Higher resistance gives lower current.

Part 4: Electrical Safety

English is the main examination language. Chinese explanations are included only to support understanding.

Short Circuit

A short circuit occurs when current flows through an unintended path with very low resistance.

短路是电流通过一条电阻很低的错误路径。

Low resistance → Large current → Heat → Fire

Overloading

Overloading occurs when too many appliances are connected to one socket or circuit.

超载是同一个插座或电路连接太多电器。

Too many appliances → Too much current

1. Fuse

A fuse is a safety device that melts and breaks the circuit when the current is too large.

保险丝在电流过大时熔断，使电路断开。

Connected in series.
Made of wire with a low melting point.
Melts when current becomes too large.
Must be replaced after melting.
Fuse melts → Circuit breaks → Appliance protected

2. Circuit Breaker

A circuit breaker automatically switches off the circuit when the current becomes too large.

断路器在电流过大时自动切断电路。

FuseCircuit Breaker
MeltsTrips or switches off
Must be replacedCan usually be reset
One-time protectionReusable protection

3. Three-Core Cable

WireColourFunction华语辅助
Live wire Brown Carries current from the power supply to the appliance. 火线把电流从电源送到电器。
Neutral wire Blue Returns current to the power supply and completes the circuit. 中性线让电流返回电源并完成电路。
Earth wire Green and yellow Carries leakage current safely to Earth. 地线把漏电电流安全导入大地。
Live brings • Neutral returns • Earth protects

4. Earth Wire and Metal Casing

Live wire touches the metal casing.
The metal casing may become live.
Earth wire carries leakage current to Earth.
A large current may cause the fuse to melt or breaker to trip.
The circuit is disconnected.
The user is protected from electric shock.
Leakage → Earth wire → Fuse/breaker acts → User protected

5. Double Insulation

Double-insulated appliances have insulating outer casings and no exposed metal parts.

They usually do not require an earth wire.

双重绝缘电器有绝缘外壳和没有外露金属部件，因此通常不需要地线。

Plastic casing + Extra insulation = Double insulation

6. Safe Use of Electricity

Do not touch switches with wet hands.
Do not overload sockets.
Replace damaged wires.
Switch off power before repairs.
Use the correct fuse rating.
Keep appliances away from water.
Do not insert objects into sockets.
Use plugs and cables in good condition.

7. Household Wiring

Household appliances are connected in parallel.

Each appliance receives the same voltage.
Each appliance can be switched independently.
If one appliance fails, others can still operate.

家庭电器采用并联连接，因为每个电器得到相同电压，可以独立开关，一个损坏时其他仍可运作。

8. Common Examination Traps

Wrong: Fuse increases current.
Correct: Fuse melts when current is too large and breaks the circuit.

Wrong: Earth wire supplies current during normal operation.
Correct: Earth wire carries leakage current to Earth during a fault.

Wrong: Circuit breaker must always be replaced after use.
Correct: A circuit breaker can usually be reset.

Wrong: Wet hands are safe because water blocks electricity.
Correct: Water on the skin lowers resistance and increases electric shock risk.

Part 5: Magnetism

English is the main examination language. Chinese explanations are included only to support understanding.

Magnet

A magnet is an object that can attract magnetic materials.

磁铁是一种能够吸引磁性物质的物体。

Magnet attracts magnetic materials

Magnetic Materials

Examples: iron, steel, nickel and cobalt.

磁性材料包括铁、钢、镍和钴。

Iron loves magnets

1. Magnetic and Non-Magnetic Materials

Magnetic MaterialsNon-Magnetic Materials
IronPlastic
SteelGlass
NickelWood
CobaltRubber
—Aluminium
—Copper

2. Magnetic Poles

Every magnet has a north pole and a south pole.

A single magnetic pole cannot exist by itself.

每块磁铁都有北极和南极。单独一个磁极不能独立存在。

Every magnet has N and S • No single pole

3. Attraction and Repulsion

Pole CombinationEffect华语辅助
North + SouthAttract异极相吸
North + NorthRepel同极相斥
South + SouthRepel同极相斥
Unlike poles attract • Like poles repel

4. Magnetic Field

A magnetic field is the region around a magnet where magnetic force can be detected.

Outside a magnet, magnetic field lines move from the north pole to the south pole.

Magnetic field lines never cross one another.

磁场是磁铁周围能够检测到磁力的区域。磁力线在磁铁外部从北极指向南极，而且不会互相交叉。

Outside magnet: N → S • Field lines never cross

5. Electromagnet

An electromagnet is a magnet produced when electric current flows through a coil.

电流流过线圈时会形成电磁铁。

Current + Coil = Electromagnet

6. Factors Affecting Electromagnet Strength

More turns of coil → Stronger electromagnet
Larger current → Stronger electromagnet
Soft iron core → Stronger electromagnet

增加线圈匝数、增大电流或使用软铁芯，都会增强电磁铁。

More coils + More current + Soft iron core = Stronger electromagnet

7. Permanent Magnet and Electromagnet

Permanent MagnetElectromagnet
Always magneticNeeds electric current
Cannot be switched off easilyCan be switched on and off
Strength is generally fixedStrength can be changed

8. Uses of Electromagnets

Scrap yard cranes
Electric bells
Relays
Loudspeakers
Electric motors
Magnetic door locks

9. Common Examination Traps

Wrong: Aluminium is a magnetic material.
Correct: Aluminium is non-magnetic in this syllabus context.

Wrong: North and north attract.
Correct: Like poles repel.

Wrong: An electromagnet works without current.
Correct: An electromagnet needs electric current.

Wrong: More coil turns make an electromagnet weaker.
Correct: More coil turns make it stronger.

Wrong: A permanent magnet can be switched off.
Correct: An electromagnet can be switched on and off.

Part 6: Experiments and Science Process Skills

This section focuses on experiment questions, variables, observations, inferences, hypotheses and conclusions.

Manipulated Variable

The factor that is deliberately changed.

实验中主动改变的因素。

MV = Change

Responding Variable

The factor that is measured or observed.

实验中被测量或观察的结果。

RV = Measure

Controlled Variable

The factor that is kept constant.

实验中保持不变的因素。

CV = Keep the same

Observation and Inference

Observation is what is seen, heard or measured. Inference is the scientific explanation.

观察是看到、听到或测量到的现象；推断是科学解释。

Observation seen • Inference explained
