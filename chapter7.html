Part 2: Electric Circuits

Open circuit: incomplete, no current.

Closed circuit: complete, current flows.

SeriesParallel
One pathMore than one path
Current is the sameCurrent splits
One bulb breaks: all go outOther branches still work

串联只有一条路径；并联有多条支路。

Part 3: Current, Voltage and Resistance

Ammeter: connected in series.

Voltmeter: connected in parallel.

Series current: I₁ = I₂ = I₃

Parallel current: I total = I₁ + I₂

Series voltage: shared.

Parallel voltage: same across each branch.

电流表串联，电压表并联；电阻越大，电流越小。

Part 4: Electrical Safety

Device / WireFunction

Earth wireCarries leakage current to Earth

火线送电，中性线回流，地线保护。超载和短路会造成大电流和发热。

Part 5: Magnetism

Magnetic materials: iron, steel, nickel and cobalt.

Unlike poles attract. Like poles repel.

Outside a magnet, field lines move from North to South.

Electromagnet: current through a coil.

More coil turns and larger current produce a stronger electromagnet.

异极相吸，同极相斥。电流流过线圈形成电磁铁。

Part 6: Experiments and SPS

MV: factor changed. RV: factor measured. CV: factor kept constant.

Conductor test: bulb lights → conductor; bulb off → insulator.

Electromagnet strength can be measured by the number of iron clips attracted.

观察写看到或测量到的现象；推断写科学解释。
