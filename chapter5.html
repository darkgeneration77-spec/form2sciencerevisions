Part 1: Water and Changes of State

Solid → Liquid: Melting

Liquid → Solid: Freezing

Liquid → Gas: Boiling / Evaporation

Gas → Liquid: Condensation

水的三种状态：固体、液体、气体。0°C 是熔点和凝固点；100°C 是沸点。

EvaporationBoiling
Occurs at any temperatureOccurs at boiling point
At liquid surfaceThroughout liquid

Part 2: Solution and Rate of Dissolving

Solute dissolves in a solvent to form a solution.

溶质溶解在溶剂中，形成溶液。

Faster dissolving: higher temperature, stirring, smaller solute size.

Saturated solution: Cannot dissolve more solute at that temperature.

Part 3: Electrolysis of Water

Electric current decomposes water into hydrogen and oxygen.

Hydrogen test: Pop sound.

Oxygen test: Glowing splint relights.

电解水会产生氢气和氧气。氢气发出啪声；氧气使带火星木条复燃。

Part 4: Water Treatment

Screening → Coagulation → Sedimentation → Filtration → Chlorination.

隔筛、凝聚、沉淀、过滤、加氯消毒。

Chlorination kills microorganisms. Filtration removes suspended particles.

Part 5: Water Supply System

Water source → Treatment plant → Storage tank → Distribution pipes → Homes.

水源经过处理后储存，再通过水管输送到住家。

Leaks waste treated water and reduce supply efficiency.

Part 6: Water Pollution and Sustainability

Sources: sewage, industrial waste, agricultural chemicals and rubbish.

Effects: death of aquatic organisms, disease, bad smell and unsafe water.

污染来源包括污水、工业废料、农药和垃圾。必须节约用水并保护水源。
